NP Membership Benefits

No annual fee for qualifying nonprofit organizations.

  • Standard listing in the chamber directory
  • Monthly member newsletter
  • Invitations to nonprofit networking sessions
  • Access to selected community workshops

Bronze Membership Benefits

MX$1,500 per year

  • All NP Membership benefits
  • Admission to monthly networking events
  • Member pricing on chamber training
  • Access to business resource guides

Silver Membership Benefits

MX$3,000 per year

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Enhanced chamber directory listing
  • Two complimentary training registrations
  • Eligibility for home-page member spotlights
  • Discounted event sponsorship opportunities

Gold Membership Benefits

MX$5,000 per year

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Priority home-page spotlight placement
  • Four complimentary training registrations
  • VIP invitations to leadership roundtables
  • Recognition at major chamber events
